A.R.K.O.V., AVC 2026-2027

Autonomous Rover with Kinematics, Odometry, and Vision

The name is subject to change but ARKOV is simply what the AVC does.
The vehicle is an autonomously driving rover with kinematics (arm) , odometry (wheel encoders) and vision(AI & video feed)
